Research

We study string theory as a theory of quantum gravity, using holographic duality as the main tool.

Quantum gravity & string theory

Quantum gravity explores gravitational physics at length scales where quantum effects become significant — a regime that neither general relativity nor quantum field theory describes on its own. String theory is currently the most promising candidate for such a theory, so understanding its physical implications is the starting point of our work.

Holographic duality

Holography states that a string theory on a (d+1)-dimensional anti-de Sitter space, together with a (9−d)-dimensional internal manifold, is equivalent to a d-dimensional conformal field theory living on the conformal boundary — the AdS/CFT correspondence. It gives one of the most concrete routes to string theory as a theory of quantum gravity: the physics of strings in AdS can be read off from the dual CFT.

Exact partition functions

Supersymmetric localization turns path integrals of superconformal field theories into finite-dimensional matrix integrals. We compute these partition functions and superconformal indices exactly, then push their large-N expansions well beyond the leading order.

Black holes & quantum corrections

Matching subleading terms — logarithmic corrections, Airy-function resummations, non-perturbative saddles — against supergravity gives a precision test of the microscopic counting behind black hole entropy in AdS.

Methods and themes

Concretely, the group works on superconformal field theories and their supersymmetric localization, large-N expansions and their non-perturbative completions, gauged and ten- or eleven-dimensional supergravities, and the black holes and black strings they support.

A recurring theme is precision holography: computing an observable exactly on the field-theory side, expanding it at large N, and identifying every term with a specific effect in the bulk — classical supergravity, higher-derivative corrections, or one-loop quantum effects.
